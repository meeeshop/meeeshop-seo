## 🎯 Key 2026 Fashion Trends to Cover in Blogs

### Denim / Jeans Trends
- **Cigarette/Stovepipe cuts** taking over from wide-leg — quiet luxury aesthetic
- **Dark indigo & clean washes** — more "expensive" and elevated look
- **Wide-leg & barrel-leg** still dominant for comfort
- **Hyper-distressed denim is OUT** — clean lines are in
- **Denim summer dresses** — lightweight, flattering, affordable
- **Cargo/utility denim** details trending on runways
- **Low-rise** making a steady comeback
- **Split-hem jeans** gaining popularity
- **Jeans + heels combo** — pointed kitten heels, mules, block-heel pumps = the "elite" combo

### Styling Trends
- **Quiet Luxury** — logo-less, clean, classic
- **Linen dresses** — beat the summer heat in style
- **All-black outfits** even in summer (7 ways to style)
- **Wedge sandals** paired with jeans/casual outfits
- **Layered earth tones** — olive, terracotta, sandy neutral
- **Pointed-toe kitten heels** with denim
- **Oversized blazer + jeans** for office-to-evening

### Care & Practical Content
- **How to wash jeans without fading** (cold water, inside out, no fabric softener)
- **Jeans care: wash every 5-10 wears only**
- **Air dry vs dryer** — heat damages denim
- **Spot cleaning tricks** for small stains
- **The weird buttons on jeans** — what are they for? (curiosity content = high clicks)

## 🔧 Implementation Guidance for modify_blog.py

### Handle-Content Matching Rule
The article `body_html` MUST match what the URL handle promises:
- Handle `how-to-cuff-jeans` → Body MUST contain: intro explaining the cuffing concept, H2s for each cuffing method (single roll, double roll, pin roll), H2 for what shoes to pair, CTA section
- Handle `how-to-remove-stinky-smell` → Body MUST contain: causes of smell (bacteria, lack of washing), methods (baking soda, vinegar spray, freezer), prevention tips
- Handle `how-to-clean-pilling` → Body MUST contain: what causes pilling (friction, fabric type), removal tools (sweater stone, razor), prevention (wash inside out, gentle cycle)

### Prompt Enhancement for Handle-Aware Content
The `_build_refresh_prompt()` function should be updated to pass the article HANDLE to AI with instructions to align content with the handle topic rather than just the featured product type.
