Fabric Care and Maintenance

Caring for your fabrics is crucial to extending the life of your clothing. Here are some tips on how to wash, remove stinky smells, prevent piling, and clean stains:

  • Wash your bottoms in cold water to prevent shrinkage and fading.
  • Use a mild detergent that's designed for the type of fabric you're washing.
  • Avoid using fabric softener or bleach, as these can damage certain fabrics.
  • To remove stinky smells, try adding a 1/2 cup of white vinegar to your wash cycle.
  • To prevent piling, avoid washing your bottoms with other clothes that have zippers or buttons.
  • To clean stains, blot the stain with a clean cloth and then apply a stain remover before washing.
