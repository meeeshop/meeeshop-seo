Universal Rules

Regardless of your body type, there are a few universal rules to keep in mind when it comes to finding the perfect top. First, focus on proportion over labels. Instead of worrying about what size you are, think about how the top fits and flatters your body. Second, don't be afraid to experiment with different styles and silhouettes. You might be surprised at what works for you. Finally, remember that confidence is key. Own your look, and you'll feel like a million bucks.

FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions about finding the perfect top for your body type:

  • Q: What's the best way to determine my body type?
  • A: The best way to determine your body type is to take your measurements and compare them to a body type chart. You can also try on different tops and see what works best for you.
  • Q: Can I wear a top that's not designed for my body type?
  • A: Yes, you can definitely wear a top that's not designed for your body type. However, keep in mind that it might not flatter your figure as much as a top that's designed specifically for your body type.
  • Q: How can I balance my hips and thighs with my top half if I'm a pear shape?
  • A: If you're a pear shape, try pairing a flowy top with a pair of high-waisted pants or a skirt. This will help balance out your hips and thighs and create a more hourglass silhouette.
  • Q: Can I wear a bold print if I'm a petite frame?
  • A: Yes, you can definitely wear a bold print if you're a petite frame. Just be sure to balance it out with a pair of neutral-colored pants or a skirt, and avoid overwhelming your frame with too much volume on top.
  • Q: How can I add some visual interest to a plain top?
  • A: There are many ways to add visual interest to a plain top, such as adding a statement necklace, a scarf, or a belt. You can also try pairing it with a pair of patterned pants or a skirt to add some contrast and visual interest.
