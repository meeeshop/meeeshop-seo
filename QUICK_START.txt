📊 THREE MODES EXPLAINED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DAILY (default)
  Scope:     Last 48 hours
  Use:       Catch new/updated products
  Schedule:  Every day 6 AM UTC
  Command:   python seo_daily.py --daily
  Time:      2-3 minutes (~50 products)

WEEKLY
  Scope:     Last 7 days
  Use:       Safety net, catch edge cases
  Schedule:  Sunday 8 AM UTC
  Command:   python seo_daily.py --weekly
  Time:      5-7 minutes (~150 products)

FORCE (⚠️ careful!)
  Scope:     All products
  Use:       Complete store refresh
  Schedule:  Manual only
  Command:   python seo_daily.py --force
  Time:      15-20 minutes (1000+ products)
